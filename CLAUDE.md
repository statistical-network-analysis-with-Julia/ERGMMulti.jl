## Project Overview

ERGMMulti.jl is a Julia package for fitting Exponential Random Graph Models (ERGMs) to multiple, multilayer, and multilevel network data. It is a port of the R `ergm.multi` package from the StatNet collection.

## Architecture

The entire package lives in a single source file: `src/ERGMMulti.jl`. It is organized into these sections:

1. **Data Structures** — `MultilayerNetwork{D}` (same actors, L layers; all layers share size, and the directedness `D` is a *type parameter* — `layers::Vector{Network{Int,D}}`, `is_directed(m)`; `add_layer!` refuses a layer of the other kind and a two-mode (bipartite) layer — `is_two_mode(net)` — with the sentence the "Not implemented" paragraphs quote; `add_layer_edge!` refuses an actor id outside `1:n` and a self-loop on a `loops=false` layer with an `ArgumentError` — never `add_edge!`'s silent `false`; `layer_network(m, ::Int)` out of range is an `ArgumentError` naming `layer_names(m)`, never a `BoundsError`), `MultiNetwork` (independent networks, descriptive), `MultilevelNetwork` (per-level networks + membership Dicts keyed by each level's own node IDs + explicit cross-level edges via `add_cross_level_edge!`). `MultiNetwork`/`MultilevelNetwork` are deliberately not parameterized: they hold networks of mixed size and directedness and never enter a fit. All three containers and `MultiERGMModel` have one-line `Base.show` methods (pinned by `sprint(show, x)` tests) — never let a public type fall back to the struct dump.
2. **Multilayer ERGM Terms** — `LayerEdges/LayerMutual/LayerTriangle` take a layer selector (Int, Vector{Int}, or Colon for pooled; a `Symbol` — or `WithinLayer(t, ::Symbol)`, `InterlayerDependence(::Symbol, …)` — is an `ArgumentError` from `_symbol_selector_error` giving `findfirst(==(:name), layer_names(m))`, never a MethodError); `WithinLayer{T,S}(term, layer)` lifts any ERGM.jl term (reusing its validated change_stat; parameterized on the term type AND the selector type so dispatch is static). `layer::Int` is `L(~t, ~A)`; a `Vector{Int}` (ranges are collected) or `:` POOLS the term — the statistic is the sum of the per-layer statistics under one coefficient, R's `L(~t, c(~A, ~B))`, labelled `L((A,B))~<term>` and pinned by `pooled_layer_terms.toml`. The constructor refuses `ERGM.Offset` (use `offsets=`) and a repeated layer index. A pooled term is validated against EVERY selected layer (the error is prefixed `layer l (:name):`) and materialized once per layer into the internal `_PerLayer{T}` (`terms::Vector{T}`, `slot[l]`; `ERGM.Extension.materialize` snapshots ONE network's attributes, so each layer needs its own twin); a pooled expanding term must expand into the same labels on every layer or the model is refused. `_layer_term`/`_selects` are the two accessors the generic `compute`/`change_stat_layer` methods use; the `Int` selector keeps its own one-line methods; `InterlayerDependence(l1,l2)` (co-occurrence) and `MultiplexMutual(l1,l2)` (cross-layer reciprocity, ordered dyads). `LayerMutual` and `MultiplexMutual` declare `ERGM.requires_directed` and refuse undirected data with ERGM's sentence (never a silent 0). Every term implements `compute(term, m)` and `change_stat_layer(term, m, l, i, j)` — the ADD-DIRECTION change for adding (i,j) in layer l, state-independent (multilayer analogue of ERGM.jl's convention). **Coefficient labels are `ergm.multi`'s**, built by `_layer_spec(sel, m)` from `layer_names(m)`: `L(A)~edges`, `L((A,B))~edges` (a pool over a list — double parentheses even for one layer; `:` lists every layer), `L(A)~mutual`, `L(A)~triangle`, `L(A&B)~edges` (`InterlayerDependence`, in the given order), `L(A,B)~mutual` (`MultiplexMutual`), and `L(A)~<ERGM label>` for `WithinLayer` (direction-aware: `L(A)~gwesp.OTP.fixed.0.5` on a directed layer), each a method of ERGM's two-argument `name(term, net)`; the one-argument `name(t)` (no network: error messages) names layers by index (`L(1)~edges`, `L((1,2))~…`, `L((:))~…`), as R does for an unnamed layer list. An offset row of `coeftable` is `offset(<label>)` (`_coef_names`). Pinned exactly by `multilayer_labels.toml` and by the label keys of every other fixture. `_change_stat_layer_all!(dest, ts::TermSet, m, l, i, j)` is the generated per-term fill (ERGM's `change_stat_all!` pattern) behind both the MPLE design and the sampler.
3. **Multilevel ERGM Terms** — `Nestedness`, `CrossLevelEdge`, `LevelHomophily`.
4. **Model construction** — `MultiERGMModel{D}(terms, m; offsets)` mirrors `ERGMModel`'s inner constructor: at least one layer and at least one within-layer dyad (`_n_within_dyads(m) > 0`; a 0- or 1-actor network is refused — "nothing to estimate or simulate" — instead of running Newton on an empty design), no layer contains a self-loop (`_refuse_layer_self_loops`, ERGM's `_refuse_self_loops` sentence; a `loops=true` layer with none is accepted), every term HAS a multilayer change statistic (`_has_multilayer_change_stat` = `hasmethod(change_stat_layer, …)`; a bare `Mutual()` or a multilevel `Nestedness` is refused with the `WithinLayer` fix — the generic `_validate_multilayer_term` fallback), every layer index in range (error names `layer_names(m)`), every `WithinLayer(t, l)` validated against layer `l` through the public `ERGM.Extension.validate_formula` (missing/partial attribute, covariate size, `requires_directed`/`requires_undirected`), the multilayer terms' own direction traits, offsets indexing terms with at least one free coefficient. Within-layer terms are then materialized through the public `ERGM.Extension.materialize` (dense attribute snapshots; expanding terms — multi-level `NodeFactor`, `Degree(0:2)` — become one `WithinLayer` per statistic, offsets remapped, an offset on an expanding term refused). `terms` is stored as an `ERGM.TermSet` with the labels in `.names`. Both `ergm_multi` and `simulate_multi_ergm` go through this constructor and also accept a prebuilt model.
5. **Estimation** — `ergm_multi()` (R name) `=== fit_ergm_multi` (the canonical `fit_<model>` name, a `const`); the development-era alias `fit_multi_ergm` was removed (rename table at the end of `docs/src/api/estimation.md`; no deprecation shims). **Default `method=:auto`** through ERGM's public `ERGM.resolve_method(method, model; context="ergm_multi")`: `:mple` when `has_dyad_dependent(model)` is false (exact MLE), `:mcmle` otherwise (R's rule); `ergm_multi(model; method, kwargs...)` is the dispatcher, `_multi_mple_estimate(model; method, maxiter, tol, se, n_boot, boot_burnin, boot_interval, rng, threaded)` the MPLE and `_multi_mcmle(model; …)` the MCMLE (both with keyword defaults). `_check_multi_keywords` reads each estimator's keywords from its signature (`_multi_keywords(Val(:mple|:mcmle))`, `Base.kwarg_decl`; `warn`/`method` excluded) and refuses one the chosen estimator does not take, ERGM's sentence ("… pass method=:mple explicitly …"); `method=:mcmle` on a dyad-independent model returns the exact MPLE relabelled, passing only `_SHARED_KEYWORDS = (:maxiter, :tol, :rng, :drop)`. `show`'s `Method:` line (`_method_gloss`) uses ERGM's wording. Tests, docstrings and the precompile workload that mean the MPLE of a dyad-dependent formula pass `method=:mple`. Real MPLE (logistic pseudo-likelihood) over the WITHIN-LAYER dyad universe with `offsets::Dict{Int,Float64}` support (fixed per-term coefficients, ergm.multi's offset mechanism); maximized with the shared `NetworkCore.newton_fit` optimizer on `NetworkCore.logistic_derivatives`. Edges-only fits reproduce logit(density) exactly. **Boundary statistics and separation** are handled as in ERGM.jl (see the section below). Non-convergence warns and is printed under the verdict. `ERGM.is_dyad_dependent` is extended to the multilayer terms (`LayerEdges` false, `WithinLayer` delegates; the conservative `true` fallback covers the rest — cross-layer terms count as dyad-dependent because the model's dyads are (layer, i, j) triples); `ERGM.has_dyad_dependent(::MultiERGMModel)` is the ONE predicate behind `show`'s caveat, `is_exact` and `approximations` — never a same-named private. The keyword vocabulary is the family's: `maxiter`, `tol`, `se`, `n_boot`, `boot_burnin`/`boot_interval` (`nothing` → `ERGM.Extension.mcmc_defaults`), `rng`; `n_sim`/`burnin`/`interval`/`rng` on the sampler and `gof`. `NetworkCore.missing_policies(ergm_multi) == (:error,)`.

   `simulate_multi_ergm` refuses a non-finite coefficient (`_require_finite_coefficients`, naming the `∓Inf`/`NaN` terms): the chain would freeze (θ'Δ = NaN, every proposal rejected). `gof` simulates a fit as fitted through `_gof_theta`: `∓Inf` → `∓_HOLD_AT_BOUND` (1e300; the chain starts at the observed network, which is at the bound, and every move off it is rejected), a dyad-independent `NaN` → 0, a dyad-dependent `NaN` and mixed `-Inf`/`+Inf` holds (`_refuse_mixed_holds`: the stand-ins would cancel in θ'Δ) refused.

   **Standard errors of the MPLE** (`se_type` on `MultiERGMResult`, reported by `NetworkCore.se_method`; `se` validated by `NetworkCore.check_se`): the keyword defaults to `nothing`, which is `:hessian` (the inverse negative pseudo-Hessian) **with ERGM.jl's MPLE presentation pattern**: for a dyad-dependent formula fitted with `method=:mple` the result has `inference_withheld == true`, `coeftable`/`show` report `NaN` z and p (a `∓Inf` coefficient keeps z = ∓Inf, p = 0), `confint` throws an `ArgumentError`, `show` prints the "not reported (NaN)" note and `approximations` records "withheld" — the naive errors under-cover (0.76–0.94 at 95 % in simulation). An explicit `se=:hessian` is the written opt-in to the naive Wald table (the old warning is printed). Dyad-independent fits are exact and never withheld. `se=:bootstrap` is a **parametric bootstrap** (simulate `n_boot` multilayer networks at the fitted coefficients *including the offsets* with `simulate_multi_ergm`, refit with the same offsets via the shared `_multi_mple_fit` core with `warn=false`, empirical covariance of the FREE coefficients), with the same API as `ERGM.mple`'s and running on the ONE shared `NetworkCore.bootstrap_cov` loop — never paste the loop in. A refit that does not converge or hits a boundary (∓Inf) is a NaN row of `fit.boot_replicates`, excluded from the covariance, warned once, noted by `show`/`approximations` — and all three carry the ERGM family's sentence verbatim, the constant `_BOOT_EXCLUSION_BIAS` ("The standard errors are conditional on a finite refit: the excluded replicates are the extreme ones, so the standard errors are biased downward."; a test asserts it equals ERGM's). The bootstrap is refused on a separated fit as well as on a `∓Inf` one. The point estimates are identical under both; only the covariance changes, and offset rows stay `NaN` under either. `show`/`approximations` read `se_type` and drop the anticonservatism caveat when a bootstrap was actually used, while keeping the *point-estimate* MPLE caveat ("biased in finite samples"), which holds either way and must never be dropped.

   **StatsAPI**: `coef`, `coefnames` (a method of `StatsAPI.coefnames`, = `_coef_names(r)`, a fresh copy equal to `coeftable(r).names`), `stderror`, `vcov`, `confint`, `loglikelihood`, `nobs`, `dof` (finite, non-offset coefficients), `aic`, `bic`, `coeftable` (a `NetworkCore.CoefficientTable`; `show` prints it, so the printed table IS the inspected one); pinned by `NetworkCore.check_statsapi(fit; required=(STATSAPI_VERBS..., :coefnames), strict=true)`.
6. **Adapters (the conversion contract)** — `as_multilayer(nets, names; report)` stores the layer networks AS GIVEN (no copy: attributes and masks stay inside; its report is lossless); `combine_networks(m; report)` builds the true BLOCK-DIAGONAL combined Network (n·L vertices, `:layer`/`:actor` vertex attributes, edges within blocks, `loops` carried) and **preserves the missing-dyad mask** — layer `l`'s masked `(i, j)` is masked at `(off+i, off+j)`, because the target can represent it (NetworkCore.jl's contract: preserve what the target holds, report what it cannot); what it cannot hold — every layer's vertex/edge/network attributes — is dropped and named by `_combine_report` (`ConversionReport(:MultilayerNetwork, :Network)`, one `record_drop!` per attribute, the reason naming the layer; no `:bipartite` entry — `add_layer!` is the guard, so no layer is two-mode and such a branch would be dead code). `split_by_layer(combined, n, L; names, report)` inverts it: remaps the mask onto the layers, refuses a masked cross-block dyad with the same `ArgumentError` as a cross-block edge, reports the combined network's own extras. `supports_missing` is `true` for all three (none reads a face value); none has a `missing=` keyword — the guard stays on the estimator. Both constructors yield the typed `MultilayerNetwork{D}`. NetworkCore.jl's `conversion_invariants.md` carries the `Network`s→`MultilayerNetwork` column; keep it in step.
   **MCMLE** (`_multi_mcmle`; modelled on ERGMCount's `count_mcmle`): ergm.multi's default for a dyad-dependent formula, and so this package's (`method=:auto`). `ergm_multi(model; method=:mcmle)` falls through to the MPLE for a dyad-independent formula (exact; `method` field `:mcmle`, `mcmc === nothing`) and refuses `se=` and the other MPLE keywords. Start: `_multi_mple_fit(warn=false, drop=drop)`. A boundary column is R's drop, not a refusal: `dropped` (free index → side) is fixed at `∓_HOLD_AT_BOUND` in the sampler's θ, warned by `_warn_multi_drop` (R's MCMLE sentence with ergm.multi's divergence), excluded from the step/stopping rule/covariance (the iteration runs over `est`, recorded as `fit.mcmc.estimated`), reported `∓Inf` with SE 0 and zero vcov rows; aliased (NaN) start columns are held at 0 and reported NaN when dyad-independent, refused unless `init=` when dyad-dependent; `drop=false` refuses before any start (`_refuse_multi_no_drop`); mixed `-Inf`/`+Inf` drops and "nothing left to estimate" are refused. Also refused (`ArgumentError`): a separated start (naming `separated_terms`), an unconverged one (unless `init=`), non-finite offsets, masked layers. **The iteration is ERGM.jl's public `_mcmle_solve`** (the loop `ERGM.mcmle` runs: Hummel-stepped Newton update, R's confidence stopping rule `termination=:confidence` with `conv_precision`/`conv_confidence` and the sample boost up to `max_n_samples`, `:hotelling` selectable, `ERGM.Extension.mcmle_covariance` Fisher + MC standard errors) — never paste the loop back; `_multi_mcmle` supplies only the `draw(θ_free, n)` closure: `_multi_mh_stats!` on THE chain `_multi_mh_chain!` continued from `current` across draws, offsets fixed in θ, the statistics carried by adding each accepted move's change statistics (`on_apply`, no network copies; pinned equal to `compute_all` on the final state), returning the free columns; `target = g_obs[free]`. Under `warn=false` the driver runs under a `NullLogger`. Result: `se_type = :mcmc`, `se_method = :fisher`, `objective = :likelihood`, `fit.mcmc` = `(convergence, mc_std_errors, samples, n_samples, burnin, interval, loglik_mc_se, start, termination, termination_p, conv_confidence, conv_precision)`; `show` prints a `Termination:` line. `samples` and the t-ratios/Hotelling p of `convergence` are the pre-step sample's (drawn at the last iterate, before the final step, as in R): the verdict is the stopping rule, and the non-convergence warning, `show` and `approximations` quote only it through `_termination_verdict(mcmc)` — ERGM.mcmle's wording: the equivalence test's p-value and γ under `:confidence`, the Hotelling p and max t-ratio only under `:hotelling` (pinned: no "t-ratio"/"Hotelling" under `:confidence`). `loglik` is the log-likelihood by path sampling (`_multi_bridge_loglik`): θ₀ = θ̂ with dyad-dependent coordinates zeroed has an exact log-likelihood (the logistic sum over the within-layer design); `log Z` is integrated to θ̂ by ERGM's public `_bridge_integrate` (composite Simpson over `bridge_rungs` segments, `threaded=true`; the callback also records each rung's batch-means variance for `loglik_mc_se`), each grid point its own chain from the observed network on its own `Xoshiro(seed)` drawn from `rng` up front (threaded, thread-count independent — pinned in a fresh process); `aic`/`bic` use it with `dof` = estimated coefficients and the BIC size = the dyads the dropped statistics leave (`start.n_kept`). A dropped dyad-INDEPENDENT statistic stays at `±1e300` in the bridge's reference model too (Δ = 0 there; `ll0` uses the overflow-safe `-log1p(exp(∓η))` form, exact at ±1e300); a dropped dyad-DEPENDENT one makes `loglik`/`aic`/`bic` NaN with `fit.mcmc.loglik_note` saying why (ERGM.jl's choice). The MCMLE's iteration cap is `mcmle_maxiter` (60, R's) because `maxiter` is Newton's. Measured on the fixture model under the confidence rule: about 1 s per fit, eight seeds within 0.80 of the band (final samples of 3 992–8 984 draws, mcse/se 0.047–0.072), Julia's eight-seed mean within 0.001–0.008 of R's long-chain fit.
7. **Simulation** — `simulate_multi_ergm`: `_multi_mh_run` → `_multi_mh_chain!` (the one chain, with an `on_apply(delta, removal)` hook after every accepted move and `on_sample(k)`): `ERGM.mh_toggle!` (the family's one Metropolis kernel; never a local loop) with `(layer, i, j)` moves restricted to within-layer dyads. `propose` draws `l ~ 1:L`, `i ~ 1:n`, `j ~ 1:n-1` bumped past `i`, swapped to `i < j` on undirected layers (`D` is a type parameter, so the branch is free); `change!(delta, move)` fills the generated `_change_stat_layer_all!` and returns `has_edge(layers[l], i, j)` (the kernel negates θ'Δ for a removal); `apply!` toggles the layer's edge; `on_sample(k)` pushes an attribute-preserving copy. `burnin`/`interval` are resolved by `_resolve_multi_mcmc` (`ERGM.Extension.mcmc_defaults` over `_n_within_dyads`). Before the chain starts, `_require_observed_layers(m, "simulate_multi_ergm")` refuses any masked layer (`require_observed(layer; face_ok=false)`, naming the layer; `missing_policies(simulate_multi_ergm) == (:error,)`) — the chain would otherwise toggle an unobserved dyad at its face value; `gof` inherits the guard. `_multi_mh_run` is the function barrier that gives the closures a concrete `TermSet{T}`. The kernel draws the rng proposal-first, then the acceptance uniform — the same order as the loop it replaced, so seeded draws are bit-identical to the pre-kernel loop (pinned LITERALLY in the tests: edge lists of seeded draws on both fixtures; do not "refresh" those literals without a reason that is not a bug). Per step: 0 B for the change-statistic fill at 8 and 34 statistics, < 4 B/step total (Graphs.jl adjacency growth), pinned.
8. **Goodness of fit** — `gof(fit)` (a method of NetworkCore.jl's generic) returns, in order: `"model statistics"`, `"layer edges"`, `"multiplexity"` (dyads tied in exactly k layers, `_multiplexity_counts`), then per layer `"degree: <name>"` (undirected) or `"idegree: <name>"`/`"odegree: <name>"` (directed) and `"esp: <name>"` (`_degree_counts`, `_esp_counts` — OTP on directed layers; `_push_distribution!` trims to the largest level seen). The helpers are pinned against brute force and against `GWESP`; a power test fits per-layer edges to two identical layers and requires the multiplexity panel to reject. No geodesic panel (disclosed).
9. **Precompile workload** — `@setup_workload`/`@compile_workload` at the end of the module (PrecompileTools; ERGM.jl's template): the documented first session on a directed AND an undirected 6-actor two-layer network (`fit_ergm_multi`, `coeftable`/`show`, `confint` of an explicit `se=:hessian` fit, `simulate_multi_ergm`, `gof`, a 3-replicate bootstrap) under a devnull console logger. First `ergm_multi` 1.90 s → 0.010 s (CHANGELOG has the full table). Keep it to the documented first calls; anything slower belongs in the tests, not here.

All term types subtype `AbstractERGMTerm` and extend the ERGM generics via `import ERGM: name, compute, change_stat, is_dyad_dependent, has_dyad_dependent, requires_directed, requires_undirected`. **No private cross-package reach-ins** — ERGM.jl's building blocks come from its extension API `ERGM.Extension` (semver-covered, called qualified); the "No private cross-package reach-ins" testset greps the source and asserts that no `ERGM._x`/`NetworkCore._x` appears at all, pins the Extension names used, and checks that the deleted local range table and warning copies stay gone.

## Boundary statistics and separation

`_multi_mple_fit` builds the within-layer design (`_multi_mple_design`) and fits it with ERGM.jl's **`ERGM.Extension.mple_fit_design(X, n_tot, n_one, names; extreme, offset, warn, note=_MULTI_MPLE_NOTES)`** — the one pseudo-likelihood fitter of the ERGM family (TERGM, ERGMRank, ERGM's own MPLE) — with `extreme = ERGM.Extension.extreme_statistics(terms, m)` restricted to the free columns: the statistics whose observed value is an end of their attainable range (R's `ergm.checkextreme.model`). The ranges are **methods of ERGM.jl's generic `ERGM.Extension.attainable_range` on a `MultilayerNetwork`** (`LayerEdges`/`LayerMutual`: the dyads of the selected layers; `InterlayerDependence`: ordered dyads on directed layers, unordered on undirected; `MultiplexMutual`: `n(n−1)`; `LayerTriangle`: `(0, Inf)`; anything else ERGM's default `(-Inf, Inf)`); a `WithinLayer` term's range is the SUM of the ranges ERGM.jl declares for the term as materialized on each selected layer (its `compute` is the sum of the per-layer values, so the pooled value is at a bound exactly when every selected layer is at the same one; no selected layer → no range). There is no local range table. The range matters where the design cannot see the bound: a term whose change statistics are all 0 (`LayerTriangle` on a layer with no two-path) used to give Newton a singular start and θ = 0 for every coefficient. `mple_fit_design` then iterates the design test (`boundary_columns(...; fixed=extreme)`), fixes the boundary columns at ∓Inf (SE 0) and fits the rest on the rows they leave untouched with the offset `η0` kept there (the exact limit; `n_kept` is the BIC size), reports aliased columns (all-zero on the rows fitted, or a linear combination of earlier ones — R's glm `NA`) as NaN, and runs NetworkCore's separation verdict on the design actually fitted, returning it with `boundary`, `aliased`, `fitted` and `kept_rows`. Its warnings close with R ergm's sentences, and "R ergm reports the same" of the drop is false of **ergm.multi 0.3.0**, whose layer operator does not propagate the range — it reports `NA` (all-zero change statistics) or warns "The MPLE does not exist!" and returns a finite ≈ −17.6 (SE ≈ 2000; section (iii) of `twolayer_layer_terms.toml`), and its MCMLE keeps that value (`boundary_multi.toml`). Never claim R "drops the same way". So `_multi_mple_fit` passes `note=_MULTI_MPLE_NOTES` (`boundary`: a closing parenthesis that states the divergence — a test pins the wording and greps for `note=_MULTI_MPLE_NOTES`; `not_varying`: ergm.multi's "not varying"; `linear_dependence`: glm's NA; `separation`: `_R_MULTI_MPLE_NOT_EXIST`), and the warnings are `mple_fit_design`'s own; there are no local warning copies and the verdict is never recomputed. `drop=false` (on both estimators; R's `control.ergm(drop=FALSE)`) refuses a boundary statistic before any fit (`_refuse_multi_no_drop`). `is_exact` is `false`; `show`/`approximations` derive the "fixed at -Inf" / "not identifiable" / log-likelihood notes from the result (`_fixed_coefficient_notes`). `se=:bootstrap` is refused on a fit with a `∓Inf` or `NaN` coefficient. R's drop written out — `offset(L(...))` at `offset.coef = -Inf` — is a model ergm.multi fits: `boundary_multi.toml` pins ERGMMulti's default fits against it.

Separation by a COMBINATION of columns (no single one at its boundary) is decided by NetworkCore's ONE separation verdict (an LP certified in exact arithmetic, R's `mple.existence`), computed once inside `ERGM.Extension.mple_fit_design` on the design actually fitted: the free columns that survive the boundary drop, on the kept rows. Offsets are not passed: a finite shift of the linear predictor cannot change the verdict (the old `_multi_separated`, which handed `η0` to `ERGM._separated` as a fixed column, is gone, and so is every `ERGM._separated`/`ERGM._warn_separated` reach-in — a test greps for them). The policy is the ecosystem's: `mple_fit_design`'s `warn_separation("ergm_multi", v, names; estimate="MPLE", note=_R_MULTI_MPLE_NOT_EXIST)` (R ergm.multi's "The MPLE does not exist!" — checked on the test design: R warns and returns finite coefficients), `converged = false, separated = true`, the flagged labels in `separated_terms` (`names[v.terms]`), `NaN` z/p for every coefficient in `coeftable`, a `NaN` `confint`, `se=:bootstrap` refused, the MCMLE start refused; `show`/`approximations` print `_separation_caveat` (NetworkCore's `separation_caveat(terms)` plus R's sentence) instead of the generic non-convergence one. `MultiERGMResult` has fifteen fields and ONE positional (inner) constructor that checks `separated == !isempty(separated_terms)` and that a separated fit is not converged — no "compatibility" constructors, so a result's flags cannot silently disagree with its numbers. The bootstrap's refits (`warn=false`) treat an unconverged, separated or dropped replicate as a NaN row.

## Not implemented (documented limitations)

Five items, disclosed **identically (the same bullet text)** in `README.md` ("Not implemented"), `docs/src/index.md` (`[Not implemented](@id not-implemented)`) and `CHANGELOG.md` ("Known limitations") — edit all three together (a testset compares the three blocks), each entry stating the exact behaviour a user gets: (i) **The MCMLE is not R's implementation** — `method=:auto` follows R (MPLE for a dyad-independent formula, MCMLE otherwise); the MCMLE uses the package's uniform proposal, has no missing-data form, fixes a boundary statistic at `∓Inf` (where ergm.multi reports `NA` or a finite value; log-likelihood `NaN` if the statistic is dyad-dependent) and is refused on a separated start; the MPLE of a dependent formula withholds z/p/intervals, `se=:bootstrap` (conditional on a finite refit, biased downward) or `se=:hessian` restore them (the testset finds the block by its opening "- **The MCMLE is not R's implementation**"); (ii) **no models over several networks** (`Networks()`, `N(~edges, ~x)`, `gofN`: networks of different size/composition) — `MultiNetwork` is descriptive, `ergm_multi` takes a `MultilayerNetwork` only; (iii) **layer logic limited to `A&B` on edges (`InterlayerDependence`) and `mutualL` (`MultiplexMutual`)** — any term within a layer or pooled over layers, but no term on a logical layer, no `twostarL`/`espL`/`dspL`/`gwespL`/`CMBL`, no edge-attribute layers (`Layer(nw, c(...))`), no `.symmetric` mixing, no bipartite layers (a two-mode layer is REFUSED by `add_layer!` — quote the sentence); (iv) **multilevel statistics are descriptive** and never enter a fit; (v) **no geodesic-distance GOF panel**.

## The MPLE derivative loop is NetworkCore.jl's, not ours

`_multi_mple_fit` used to carry its own logistic loop with `hess .-= (pr*(1-pr)) .* (x * x')` inside it: a fresh `pf×pf` outer product on every one of the `n_dyads` rows of every Newton evaluation — **649 KB and 0.80 ms per evaluation** on a 2-layer, 40-actor design. The pseudo-likelihood over the within-layer dyads **is** a logistic likelihood with an offset, so the derivatives now come from the shared **`NetworkCore.logistic_derivatives(Xf, y; offset=η0)`** (the same builder ERGM's MPLE, TERGM's CMPLE and ERGMRank's swap MPLE run on; it moved from ERGM.jl to NetworkCore.jl in 0.2): workspaces allocated once, `η = Xfβ` by gemv and `−H = Xf'WXf` by gemm, **304 bytes and 0.10 ms per evaluation** — 7.8x faster, and the allocations no longer scale with the dyad count. `_multi_mple_design(m, terms, offsets, free) -> (Xf, y, η0)` is the design builder, factored out so the `@allocated` regression test can measure the closure the *fitter* builds. **Never paste the loop back in**; if it needs a feature, add it to `NetworkCore.logistic_derivatives`. The summation order moves from row-wise accumulation to BLAS, so the arithmetic is not bit-identical — but the *fit* is: measured against the old loop on the same design, **max|Δθ| = 1.1e-16**, one ulp. (Newton's last step is quadratically convergent; a last-ulp difference in the gradient and Hessian does not move its fixed point.) The golden fixture agrees at 5.0e-13 in the coefficients and 1.4e-11 in the SEs — unmoved, against a 1e-6 exact-MLE tolerance.

## Golden fixtures (statnet ergm.multi)

**`multilayer_labels.toml`** (`test/fixtures/r/multilayer_labels.R`, seconds, no random draw) pins the COEFFICIENT LABELS: `names(summary())` of seventeen terms (nineteen statistics) on three named directed layers `friend`/`advice`/`cowork` — pools over a subset, over every layer and over one layer (`L((advice))~edges`), both orders of a layer pair (`L(cowork&friend)~edges`), `mutualL` in both orders, `nodefactor`/`gwesp`/`nodecov`/`idegree` inside `L()` — and six on two undirected layers, compared exactly with the statistics at 1e-10, plus `names(coef())` of an MPLE fit with two offset terms (`offset(L(advice)~nodematch.g)`). The other fixtures' label keys (`stat_names`, `mple_term_names`, `term_names`, …) are asserted equal to the model's names on layers named `A`/`B` — no relabelling map.

**`multilayer_mcmle.toml`** (`test/fixtures/r/multilayer_mcmle.R`, a few minutes on 3 cores) is the MCMLE fixture, on its OWN data: two seeded 16-actor directed layers with reciprocity in A and overlap between A and B (frozen as edge lists), model `L(~edges,~A) + L(~edges,~B) + L(~mutual,~A) + L(~edges,~A&B) + L(~gwesp(0.25,fixed=TRUE),~B)`, fitted by ergm.multi at `control.ergm` defaults under nine seeds (per-seed coefficients/SEs, mean, sd, bridge log-likelihoods), one long-chain fit (`hp_*`; the script `stopifnot`s the nine-seed mean agrees with it) and the MPLE. Adding the exact refit to the script left every Monte-Carlo value byte-identical (each fit is seeded). Never widen the band to make a seed pass.

**`boundary_multi.toml`** (`test/fixtures/r/boundary_multi.R`, ~20 s) pins R's drop on three designs with a statistic at its bound: (a) `L(~triangle,~A)` on an undirected perfect matching (all change statistics zero — only the attainable range sees it), (b) `L(~edges,~A&B)` = 0 with `L(~mutual,~A)`, (c) a dyad-independent `L(~nodematch("g"),~A)` = 0 with `L(~mutual,~A)` (so the log-likelihood is computed on both sides). The other three TOML fixtures are all generated from the SAME frozen layers (seed 20260713, the `grp` attribute; the testset asserts the edge lists agree), all loaded with NetworkCore.jl's `load_golden` (which throws without `[provenance]`), all regenerable with `Rscript test/fixtures/r/<name>.R > test/fixtures/<name>.toml` (ergm.multi 0.3.0 is installed).

**`twolayer_ergm_multi.toml`** (`test/fixtures/r/twolayer_ergm_multi.R`) licenses the dyad universe, the logistic kernel and the offset mechanism. R model: `Networks(list(net1, net2)) ~ N(~edges + nodematch("grp"), ~factor(.NetworkID) - 1)` — per-layer edges and nodematch — which is exactly `[LayerEdges(1), LayerEdges(2), WithinLayer(NodeMatch(:grp), 1), WithinLayer(NodeMatch(:grp), 2)]` here. ## Conventions

- Julia 1.12+ required (see `Project.toml` compat).
- `MultilayerNetwork{D}`/`MultiERGMModel{D}`/`MultiERGMResult{D}` are parameterized by directedness; layers are `Network{Int,D}`.
- Layer and network names use `Symbol` (e.g., `:friendship`, `:advice`).
- Each ERGM term struct implements `name(t)::String` (and, where the label depends on the data, `name(t, m)`) and `compute(t, data)::Float64`.
- Keyword vocabulary: `maxiter`, `n_sim`, `rng`, `n_boot`; `burnin`/`interval` default to `nothing` (dyad-scaled); the adapters take `report=false`.
- CI: `.github/workflows/CI.yml` and `Documentation.yml` carry the ecosystem's one generated step, "Reconstruct the ecosystem layout from [sources]" (synced from the site's `tools/sync_workflows.jl`; never hand-edit it or add a literal clone list). The testset "Workflows reconstruct the ecosystem layout from [sources]" extracts that step from both files and RUNS it with `LAYOUT_CHECK_ONLY=true` (clearing `JULIA_LOAD_PATH`/`JULIA_PROJECT`, which `Pkg.test`'s sandbox sets and which would hide the `TOML` stdlib), asserting it finds exactly `ERGMMulti.jl`, `ERGM.jl`, `NetworkCore.jl`; it also pins the docs `[sources]` and `[compat]`. Outside the sibling layout (a lone checkout or a registry install, where `[sources]` is not used) the step cannot run: when NONE of the sibling checkouts is beside the package (`sibling_checkouts(pkgdir, siblings)`, a test-file helper pinned on a `mktempdir`), the testset `@info`s why and `@test_skip`s the run; with at least one sibling present it runs and the exact-set comparison fails on a broken layout. The static checks of the package's own files run in both cases. Subprocesses in the suite go through `run_fresh(cmd; expect_lines)`, which prints the child's stdout and stderr when it fails or its output has the wrong shape. One matrix cell (Julia 1 / ubuntu) runs on `JULIA_NUM_THREADS=4` so the bootstrap's thread-independence pin means something.
- Aqua (`Aqua.test_all(ERGMMulti; ambiguities=false)` plus `Test.detect_ambiguities(ERGMMulti, ERGM, NetworkCore)` empty) is part of the suite; test-only `[extras]` carry `[compat]` entries.
- Multilayer change statistics use `change_stat_layer(t, m, layer, i, j)` (add-direction, state-independent); brute-force verified in tests.
- Functions that mutate use the `!` suffix convention (`add_layer!`, `add_layer_edge!`).
- Exports are declared at the top of the module; no re-exports from dependencies (the StatsAPI verbs are re-exported so `coef(fit)` works with `using ERGMMulti` alone).
- **Every export carries a docstring with a fenced ```julia example that runs with nothing but `using ERGMMulti`** (an example that needs `ERGM`, `NetworkCore`, `Random` or `LinearAlgebra` says so itself); the testset "Every export has a docstring with a runnable example" walks `Base.Docs.meta(ERGMMulti)` and executes every block, and the ERGMMulti-owned docstrings on the foreign generics (`coef`…`bic`, `confint`, `coeftable`, `gof`) count.
