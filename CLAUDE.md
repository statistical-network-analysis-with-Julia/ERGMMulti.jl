## Project Overview

ERGMMulti.jl is a Julia package for fitting Exponential Random Graph Models (ERGMs) to multiple, multilayer, and multilevel network data. It is a port of the R `ergm.multi` package from the StatNet collection.

## Architecture

The entire package lives in a single source file: `src/ERGMMulti.jl`. It is organized into these sections:

1. **Data Structures** — `MultilayerNetwork{D}` (same actors, L layers; all layers share size, and the directedness `D` is a *type parameter* — `layers::Vector{Network{Int,D}}`, `is_directed(m)`; `add_layer!` refuses a layer of the other kind and a two-mode (bipartite) layer — `is_two_mode(net)` — with the sentence the "Not implemented" paragraphs quote; `add_layer_edge!` refuses an actor id outside `1:n` and a self-loop on a `loops=false` layer with an `ArgumentError` — never `add_edge!`'s silent `false`; `layer_network(m, ::Int)` out of range is an `ArgumentError` naming `layer_names(m)`, never a `BoundsError`), `MultiNetwork` (independent networks, descriptive), `MultilevelNetwork` (per-level networks + membership Dicts keyed by each level's own node IDs + explicit cross-level edges via `add_cross_level_edge!`). `MultiNetwork`/`MultilevelNetwork` are deliberately not parameterized: they hold networks of mixed size and directedness and never enter a fit. All three containers and `MultiERGMModel` have one-line `Base.show` methods (pinned by `sprint(show, x)` tests) — never let a public type fall back to the struct dump.
2. **Multilayer ERGM Terms** — `LayerEdges/LayerMutual/LayerTriangle` take a layer selector (Int, Vector{Int}, or Colon for pooled; a `Symbol` — or `WithinLayer(t, ::Symbol)`, `InterlayerDependence(::Symbol, …)` — is an `ArgumentError` from `_symbol_selector_error` giving `findfirst(==(:name), layer_names(m))`, never a MethodError); `WithinLayer{T}(term, l)` lifts any ERGM.jl term (reusing its validated change_stat; parameterized on the term type so dispatch is static); `InterlayerDependence(l1,l2)` (co-occurrence) and `MultiplexMutual(l1,l2)` (cross-layer reciprocity, ordered dyads). `LayerMutual` and `MultiplexMutual` declare `ERGM.requires_directed` and refuse undirected data with ERGM's sentence (never a silent 0). Every term implements `compute(term, m)` and `change_stat_layer(term, m, l, i, j)` — the ADD-DIRECTION change for adding (i,j) in layer l, state-independent (multilayer analogue of ERGM.jl's convention). `name(t::WithinLayer, m)` is the direction-aware label (`L1.gwesp.OTP.fixed.0.5` on a directed layer), a method of ERGM's two-argument `name(term, net)`. `_change_stat_layer_all!(dest, ts::TermSet, m, l, i, j)` is the generated per-term fill (ERGM's `change_stat_all!` pattern) behind both the MPLE design and the sampler.
3. **Multilevel ERGM Terms** — `Nestedness`, `CrossLevelEdge`, `LevelHomophily`.
4. **Model construction** — `MultiERGMModel{D}(terms, m; offsets)` mirrors `ERGMModel`'s inner constructor: at least one layer and at least one within-layer dyad (`_n_within_dyads(m) > 0`; a 0- or 1-actor network is refused — "nothing to estimate or simulate" — instead of running Newton on an empty design), no layer contains a self-loop (`_refuse_layer_self_loops`, ERGM's `_refuse_self_loops` sentence; a `loops=true` layer with none is accepted), every term HAS a multilayer change statistic (`_has_multilayer_change_stat` = `hasmethod(change_stat_layer, …)`; a bare `Mutual()` or a multilevel `Nestedness` is refused with the `WithinLayer` fix — the generic `_validate_multilayer_term` fallback), every layer index in range (error names `layer_names(m)`), every `WithinLayer(t, l)` validated against layer `l` through the public `ERGM._validate_formula` (missing/partial attribute, covariate size, `requires_directed`/`requires_undirected`), the multilayer terms' own direction traits, offsets indexing terms with at least one free coefficient. Within-layer terms are then materialized through the public `ERGM._materialize` (dense attribute snapshots; expanding terms — multi-level `NodeFactor`, `Degree(0:2)` — become one `WithinLayer` per statistic, offsets remapped, an offset on an expanding term refused). `terms` is stored as an `ERGM.TermSet` with the labels in `.names`. Both `ergm_multi` and `simulate_multi_ergm` go through this constructor and also accept a prebuilt model.
5. **Estimation** — `ergm_multi()` (R name) `=== fit_ergm_multi` (the canonical `fit_<model>` name, a `const`); `fit_multi_ergm` is a deprecated wrapper (`Base.depwarn`). Real MPLE (logistic pseudo-likelihood) over the WITHIN-LAYER dyad universe with `offsets::Dict{Int,Float64}` support (fixed per-term coefficients, ergm.multi's offset mechanism); maximized with the shared `Networks.newton_fit` optimizer on `Networks.logistic_derivatives`. Edges-only fits reproduce logit(density) exactly. **Boundary statistics and separation** are handled as in ERGM.jl (see the section below). Non-convergence warns and is printed under the verdict. `ERGM.is_dyad_dependent` is extended to the multilayer terms (`LayerEdges` false, `WithinLayer` delegates; the conservative `true` fallback covers the rest — cross-layer terms count as dyad-dependent because the model's dyads are (layer, i, j) triples); `ERGM.has_dyad_dependent(::MultiERGMModel)` is the ONE predicate behind `show`'s caveat, `is_exact` and `approximations` — never a same-named private. The keyword vocabulary is the family's: `maxiter`, `tol`, `se`, `n_boot`, `boot_burnin`/`boot_interval` (`nothing` → `ERGM._mcmc_defaults`), `rng`; `n_sim`/`burnin`/`interval`/`rng` on the sampler and `gof`. `Networks.missing_policies(ergm_multi) == (:error,)`.

   `simulate_multi_ergm` and `gof` refuse a non-finite coefficient (`_require_finite_coefficients`, naming the `∓Inf`/`NaN` terms): a boundary-dropped fit cannot be simulated from — the chain would freeze (θ'Δ = NaN, every proposal rejected) and `gof` would report p = 1 everywhere.

   **Standard errors** (`se_type` on `MultiERGMResult`, reported by `Networks.se_method`; `se` validated by `Networks.check_se`): `se=:hessian` (default) is the inverse negative pseudo-Hessian — anticonservative for any dyad-dependent term over the `(layer, i, j)` dyad universe. `se=:bootstrap` is a **parametric bootstrap** (simulate `n_boot` multilayer networks at the fitted coefficients *including the offsets* with `simulate_multi_ergm`, refit with the same offsets via the shared `_multi_mple_fit` core with `warn=false`, empirical covariance of the FREE coefficients), with the same API as `ERGM.mple`'s and running on the ONE shared `Networks.bootstrap_cov` loop — never paste the loop in. A refit that does not converge or hits a boundary (∓Inf) is a NaN row of `fit.boot_replicates`, excluded from the covariance, warned once, noted by `show`/`approximations` (ERGM's round-3 pattern). The point estimates are identical under both; only the covariance changes, and offset rows stay `NaN` under either. `show`/`approximations` read `se_type` and drop the anticonservatism caveat when a bootstrap was actually used, while keeping the *point-estimate* MPLE caveat, which holds either way.

   **StatsAPI**: `coef`, `stderror`, `vcov`, `confint`, `loglikelihood`, `nobs`, `dof` (finite, non-offset coefficients), `aic`, `bic`, `coeftable` (a `Networks.CoefficientTable`; `show` prints it, so the printed table IS the inspected one); pinned by `Networks.check_statsapi(fit; strict=true)`.
6. **Adapters (the conversion contract)** — `as_multilayer(nets, names; report)` stores the layer networks AS GIVEN (no copy: attributes and masks stay inside; its report is lossless); `combine_networks(m; report)` builds the true BLOCK-DIAGONAL combined Network (n·L vertices, `:layer`/`:actor` vertex attributes, edges within blocks, `loops` carried) and **preserves the missing-dyad mask** — layer `l`'s masked `(i, j)` is masked at `(off+i, off+j)`, because the target can represent it (Networks.jl's contract: preserve what the target holds, report what it cannot); what it cannot hold — every layer's vertex/edge/network attributes — is dropped and named by `_combine_report` (`ConversionReport(:MultilayerNetwork, :Network)`, one `record_drop!` per attribute, the reason naming the layer; no `:bipartite` entry — `add_layer!` is the guard, so no layer is two-mode and such a branch would be dead code). `split_by_layer(combined, n, L; names, report)` inverts it: remaps the mask onto the layers, refuses a masked cross-block dyad with the same `ArgumentError` as a cross-block edge, reports the combined network's own extras. `supports_missing` is `true` for all three (none reads a face value); none has a `missing=` keyword — the guard stays on the estimator. Both constructors yield the typed `MultilayerNetwork{D}`. Networks.jl's `conversion_invariants.md` carries the `Network`s→`MultilayerNetwork` column; keep it in step.
7. **Simulation** — `simulate_multi_ergm`: `ERGM.mh_toggle!` (the family's one Metropolis kernel; never a local loop) with `(layer, i, j)` moves restricted to within-layer dyads. `propose` draws `l ~ 1:L`, `i ~ 1:n`, `j ~ 1:n-1` bumped past `i`, swapped to `i < j` on undirected layers (`D` is a type parameter, so the branch is free); `change!(delta, move)` fills the generated `_change_stat_layer_all!` and returns `has_edge(layers[l], i, j)` (the kernel negates θ'Δ for a removal); `apply!` toggles the layer's edge; `on_sample(k)` pushes an attribute-preserving copy. `burnin`/`interval` are resolved by `_resolve_multi_mcmc` (`ERGM._mcmc_defaults` over `_n_within_dyads`). Before the chain starts, `_require_observed_layers(m, "simulate_multi_ergm")` refuses any masked layer (`require_observed(layer; face_ok=false)`, naming the layer; `missing_policies(simulate_multi_ergm) == (:error,)`) — the chain would otherwise toggle an unobserved dyad at its face value; `gof` inherits the guard. `_multi_mh_run` is the function barrier that gives the closures a concrete `TermSet{T}`. The kernel draws the rng proposal-first, then the acceptance uniform — the same order as the loop it replaced, so seeded draws are bit-identical to the pre-kernel loop (pinned LITERALLY in the tests: edge lists of seeded draws on both fixtures; do not "refresh" those literals without a reason that is not a bug). Per step: 0 B for the change-statistic fill at 8 and 34 statistics, < 4 B/step total (Graphs.jl adjacency growth), pinned.
8. **Precompile workload** — `@setup_workload`/`@compile_workload` at the end of the module (PrecompileTools; ERGM.jl's template): the documented first session on a directed AND an undirected 6-actor two-layer network (`fit_ergm_multi`, `coeftable`/`confint`/`show`, `simulate_multi_ergm`, `gof`, a 3-replicate bootstrap) under a devnull console logger. First `ergm_multi` 1.90 s → 0.010 s (CHANGELOG has the full table). Keep it to the documented first calls; anything slower belongs in the tests, not here.

All term types subtype `AbstractERGMTerm` and extend the ERGM generics via `import ERGM: name, compute, change_stat, is_dyad_dependent, has_dyad_dependent, requires_directed, requires_undirected`. **Cross-package reach-ins must target `public` bindings** — a testset greps the source for `ERGM._x`/`Networks._x` and asserts `Base.ispublic`; the four boundary/separation helpers are `@test_broken` until ERGM.jl declares them public.

## Boundary statistics and separation

`_multi_mple_fit` runs ERGM.jl's attainable-range test (`ERGM._boundary_columns_iterated`, R ergm's `ergm.checkextreme.model`) on the FREE columns of the within-layer design before any Newton step — offset columns are fixed and never tested. A column at its boundary gets R ergm's default `drop=TRUE` semantics — which **ergm.multi 0.3.0 does not inherit**: its layer operator does not propagate the range to `ergm.checkextreme.model`, so R warns "The MPLE does not exist!" and returns a finite ≈ −17.6 (SE ≈ 2000) for the same design while agreeing on the finite coefficients (frozen as section (iii) of `twolayer_layer_terms.toml`; never claim R "drops the same way"): the local `_warn_multi_boundary` prints R's sentence through ERGM's `public` `_warn_boundary(...; context="ergm_multi", note=)` — the parenthesis is the one clause ERGMMulti supplies ("observed statistic(s) duplex.1.2 are at their smallest attainable values. Their coefficients will be fixed at -Inf …") with a closing parenthesis that states that divergence — NOT `ERGM._warn_boundary`, whose "R ergm reports the same" is true of `ergm` and false of `ergm.multi` (a test pins the wording and greps the source for the reach-in), the coefficient is fixed at ∓Inf with SE 0 / z ∓Inf / p 0, and the remaining free columns are refit on the rows every dropped column leaves untouched with the offset `η0` kept on those rows (the exact limit of the pseudo-likelihood); `n_kept` is the BIC sample size and `dof` counts finite coefficients. `is_exact` is `false`; `show`/`approximations` derive a "fixed at -Inf" note from the coefficients themselves. `se=:bootstrap` is refused (`ArgumentError`) on such a fit — nothing can be simulated at an infinite coefficient.

Separation by a COMBINATION of columns (no single one at its boundary) is caught by ERGM.jl's asymptote test `ERGM._separated` (R's LP verdict "The MPLE does not exist!"), applied by `_multi_separated` with OR without offsets: `_separated` recomputes the linear predictor as `X * θ`, so the offset contribution `η0` is passed as one more column with a fixed coefficient of 1 and SE 0 (`hcat(Xf, η0)`, `vcat(θ, 1.0)`, `vcat(se, 0.0)`, the derivative closure reading `view(θa, 1:pf)`); the tests pin the separated design under three offset dictionaries and a well-posed offset fit as unflagged. A separated fit prints `ERGM._warn_separated("ergm_multi")`, returns `converged = false, separated = true` (a field of `MultiERGMResult`, whose ONLY constructor is the positional one over all eleven fields — no "compatibility" constructors that default `se_type`/`boot_replicates`/`separated`, so a result's flags cannot silently disagree with its numbers), and `show`/`approximations` print R's sentence instead of the generic non-convergence one. The bootstrap's refits (`warn=false`) treat an unconverged, separated or dropped replicate as a NaN row.

## Not implemented (documented limitations)

Four items, disclosed **identically** in `README.md` ("Not implemented"), `docs/src/index.md` (`[Not implemented](@id not-implemented)`) and `CHANGELOG.md` ("Not implemented (known limitations, disclosed)") — edit all three together, each entry stating the exact behaviour a user gets: (i) **no MCMLE** — every fit is an MPLE, `is_exact == false` and a printed caveat for a dyad-dependent formula, `se=:bootstrap` the remedy, no `method=` keyword; (ii) **no covariate-driven multi-network models** (`N(~edges, ~x)`) — `MultiNetwork` is descriptive, `ergm_multi` takes a `MultilayerNetwork` only, per-layer/pooled parameterisations are what exist; (iii) **layer logic limited to `A&B` (`InterlayerDependence`) and `mutualL` (`MultiplexMutual`)** — no `twostarL`/`espL`/`dspL`/`gwespL`/`CMBL`, no general layer-logic formulas, no bipartite layers (a two-mode layer is REFUSED by `add_layer!` — quote the sentence, never "nothing to misuse silently"); `WithinLayer` covers single-layer forms; (iv) **multilevel statistics are descriptive** and never enter a fit (a multilevel term in a multilayer formula is refused by the constructor).

## The MPLE derivative loop is ERGM.jl's, not ours (review finding 15)

`_multi_mple_fit` used to carry its own logistic loop with `hess .-= (pr*(1-pr)) .* (x * x')` inside it: a fresh `pf×pf` outer product on every one of the `n_dyads` rows of every Newton evaluation — **649 KB and 0.80 ms per evaluation** on a 2-layer, 40-actor design. The pseudo-likelihood over the within-layer dyads **is** a logistic likelihood with an offset, so the derivatives now come from the shared **`Networks.logistic_derivatives(Xf, y; offset=η0)`** (the same builder ERGM's MPLE, TERGM's CMPLE and ERGMRank's swap MPLE run on; it moved from ERGM.jl to Networks.jl in 0.2): workspaces allocated once, `η = Xfβ` by gemv and `−H = Xf'WXf` by gemm, **304 bytes and 0.10 ms per evaluation** — 7.8x faster, and the allocations no longer scale with the dyad count. `_multi_mple_design(m, terms, offsets, free) -> (Xf, y, η0)` is the design builder, factored out so the `@allocated` regression test can measure the closure the *fitter* builds. **Never paste the loop back in**; if it needs a feature, add it to `Networks.logistic_derivatives`. The summation order moves from row-wise accumulation to BLAS, so the arithmetic is not bit-identical — but the *fit* is: measured against the old loop on the same design, **max|Δθ| = 1.1e-16**, one ulp. (Newton's last step is quadratically convergent; a last-ulp difference in the gradient and Hessian does not move its fixed point.) The golden fixture agrees at 5.0e-13 in the coefficients and 1.4e-11 in the SEs — unmoved, against a 1e-6 exact-MLE tolerance.

## Golden fixtures (statnet ergm.multi)

Two TOML fixtures, both generated from the SAME frozen layers (seed 20260713, the `grp` attribute; the testset asserts the edge lists agree), both loaded with Networks.jl's `load_golden` (which throws without `[provenance]`), both regenerable with `Rscript test/fixtures/r/<name>.R > test/fixtures/<name>.toml` (ergm.multi 0.3.0 is installed).

**`twolayer_ergm_multi.toml`** (`test/fixtures/r/twolayer_ergm_multi.R`) licenses the dyad universe, the logistic kernel and the offset mechanism. R model: `Networks(list(net1, net2)) ~ N(~edges + nodematch("grp"), ~factor(.NetworkID) - 1)` — per-layer edges and nodematch — which is exactly `[LayerEdges(1), LayerEdges(2), WithinLayer(NodeMatch(:grp), 1), WithinLayer(NodeMatch(:grp), 2)]` here. Both layers are frozen as edge lists.

Every term is dyad-independent, so the likelihood factorizes over the `(layer, i, j)` dyads and both packages compute the same **exact MLE**. ERGMMulti.jl reproduces a tightly-converged (`epsilon = 1e-14`) R `glm` to **~1e-13** in the coefficients and **~1e-11** in the standard errors. (ergm's own MPLE `glm` stops at the default `epsilon = 1e-8` and is 1.3e-9 / 4.8e-6 from the optimum — measured, and the as-shipped tolerances are set from the measurement.)

**Offsets are validated against the bug that actually happens.** A second frozen fit pins the nodematch coefficients at `offset.coef = (0.75, 0.40)`; ERGMMulti.jl's `offsets=Dict(3=>0.75, 4=>0.40)` reproduces it to ~1e-13. The fixture *also* freezes the fit of the same model with **no nodematch term at all** — where a fit lands if the offsets are dropped from the *linear predictor* rather than merely from the parameter vector — and the testset asserts the offset fit is nowhere near it (−1.418 vs −1.030). A test that only checked "matches R" could pass while both sides silently did nothing.

**`twolayer_layer_terms.toml`** (`test/fixtures/r/twolayer_layer_terms.R`) licenses the layer terms' STATISTICS and the cross-layer/mutual CHANGE STATISTICS, on DIRECTED layers in sections (i)–(iii) and on UNDIRECTED layers in section (iv). The layers are wrapped as `lnw <- Layer(list(A = net1, B = net2))`. (i) `summary(lnw ~ L(~edges,~A) + L(~edges,~B) + L(~mutual,~A) + L(~triangle,~A) + L(~edges,~A&B) + mutualL(Ls=list(~A,~B)) + L(~gwesp(0.5,fixed=TRUE),~A) + L(~ostar(2),~B) + L(~nodematch("grp"),~B) + L(~twopath,~A))` is frozen exactly (tolerance 1e-10) and asserted against `LayerEdges`, `LayerMutual`, `LayerTriangle`, `InterlayerDependence`, `MultiplexMutual` and `WithinLayer(GWESP/OStar/NodeMatch/TwoPath)`. Both cross-layer terms count ORDERED dyads in R (checked on a 4-actor example where ordered and unordered counting differ, 3 vs 2) and here. (iii) The BOUNDARY design (6-actor cycle / reversed cycle, `L(A&B)~edges == 0`): R's finite coefficients (`boundary_r_coefficients`, the third ≈ −17.58 with SE 1996, `boundary_mple_exists_warning = true`) freeze the documented divergence; ERGMMulti's `-Inf` fit is held to `boundary_finite_coefficients` (= logit(6/18)) at 1e-6. (ii) The DYAD-DEPENDENT MPLE `ergm(lnw ~ L(~edges,~A) + L(~edges,~B) + L(~edges,~A&B) + L(~mutual,~A), estimate="MPLE")` — coefficients and SEs as shipped, plus the same pseudo-likelihood refit at `glm` epsilon 1e-14 on `ergmMPLE(output="matrix")`'s own compressed design (whose weights are asserted to sum to the 760 within-layer dyads). The MPLE is a deterministic function of the change-statistic design, so `ergm_multi(m, [LayerEdges(1), LayerEdges(2), InterlayerDependence(1,2), LayerMutual(1)])` is held to the exact refit at 1e-6 (observed ~9e-8 / ~7e-9); the as-shipped tolerances are 20x the measured glm slack. (iv) The UNDIRECTED branch (`und_*` keys): two undirected 12-actor layers drawn from the same seed stream right after the directed ones (frozen as `i < j` edge lists, `grp` alternating), `summary()` of `L(~edges,~A/B)`, `L(~edges,~A&B)`, `L(~triangle,~A)`, `L(~gwesp(0.5,fixed=TRUE),~A)`, `L(~nodematch("grp"),~B)`, `L(~kstar(2),~B)`, `L(~gwdegree(0.3,fixed=TRUE),~B)` at 1e-10 (asserted against `LayerEdges`, `InterlayerDependence` — UNORDERED co-occurrences here — `LayerTriangle`, `WithinLayer(GWESP/NodeMatch/Kstar/GWDegree)`, and the `L1.gwesp.fixed.0.5` label), and the dyad-dependent MPLE `edges A + edges B + A&B + triangle A` as shipped plus the exact refit on `ergmMPLE`'s design (weights summing to 132 = `nobs`, the unordered within-layer dyads); observed ~2e-11 / ~6e-10 against 1e-6.

## Conventions

- Julia 1.12+ required (see `Project.toml` compat).
- `MultilayerNetwork{D}`/`MultiERGMModel{D}`/`MultiERGMResult{D}` are parameterized by directedness; layers are `Network{Int,D}`.
- Layer and network names use `Symbol` (e.g., `:friendship`, `:advice`).
- Each ERGM term struct implements `name(t)::String` (and, where the label depends on the data, `name(t, m)`) and `compute(t, data)::Float64`.
- Keyword vocabulary: `maxiter`, `n_sim`, `rng`, `n_boot`; `burnin`/`interval` default to `nothing` (dyad-scaled); the adapters take `report=false`.
- CI: `.github/workflows/CI.yml` and `Documentation.yml` clone exactly the `[sources]` siblings (`Networks ERGM`, foundation first) — a testset parses both and fails on drift; one matrix cell (Julia 1 / ubuntu) runs on `JULIA_NUM_THREADS=4` so the bootstrap's thread-independence pin means something.
- Multilayer change statistics use `change_stat_layer(t, m, layer, i, j)` (add-direction, state-independent); brute-force verified in tests.
- Functions that mutate use the `!` suffix convention (`add_layer!`, `add_layer_edge!`).
- Exports are declared at the top of the module; no re-exports from dependencies (the StatsAPI verbs are re-exported so `coef(fit)` works with `using ERGMMulti` alone).
- **Every export carries a docstring with a fenced ```julia example that runs with nothing but `using ERGMMulti`** (an example that needs `ERGM`, `Networks`, `Random` or `LinearAlgebra` says so itself); the testset "Every export has a docstring with a runnable example" walks `Base.Docs.meta(ERGMMulti)` and executes every block, and the ERGMMulti-owned docstrings on the foreign generics (`coef`…`bic`, `confint`, `coeftable`, `gof`) count. Comments of the form `# 1.0` state the value the line returns — keep them true.
- Docs layout: `docs/src/guide/estimation.md` is organised by what a user meets (*Boundary statistics and separation*, *Non-convergence*, *Standard errors*, *Missing dyads*, *Defaults*) and quotes the exact warning sentences; `guide/terms.md` carries *Validation* and the *Correspondence with `ergm.multi`* table (term → R term → fixture) — a new term or fixture adds a row; `api/*.md` list every export (Documenter's `checkdocs=:exports` fails the build otherwise).
